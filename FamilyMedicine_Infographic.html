Premarital Care

ILOs of This Chapter

  • Identify importance of premarital care
  • Identify components of premarital care
  • Practice premarital care counselling

Overview

Marriage is the fundamental building block of society, and the cornerstone in building emotional, healthy and family relationships. The healthy marriage guarantees protecting the family members from the hereditary and infectious diseases; thus building a happy and stable family.

DEFINITION
Premarital care is the specific service provided for the couples before marriage. By caring for the health of present children and adolescents, we are actually starting to provide care for the future generation.
TIMING
Couples planning to marry are advised to conduct premarital screening at least three months before the marriage date. The premarital compatibility certificate is valid for only six months.

Definition &amp; Goal of Antenatal Care

DEFINITION
Antenatal care (ANC) is an umbrella term used to describe the medical procedures and care that are carried out during pregnancy. It is the care a woman receives throughout her pregnancy and is important in helping to ensure a healthy pregnancy state and safe childbirth.

Goal of Antenatal Care

🎯 Ultimate Goal
Reduce maternal and perinatal mortality and morbidity rates.
Prepare the woman for labor, lactation, and care of her infant. Detect early and treat properly complicated conditions that could endanger the life or impair the health of the mother or the fetus.
How This is Achieved
  1. Detection & management of complications (medical, surgical, obstetrical)
  2. Promote physical, mental, social health of mother & baby — nutrition, hygiene, birthing process
  3. Develop birth preparedness & complication readiness plan
  4. Prepare mother to breastfeed successfully, experience normal puerperium, and care for child

Introduction &amp; Pre-pregnancy Preparation

KEY POINT
Maternal nutrition before and during pregnancy influences the course of pregnancy, fetal development, and the child's health in early and adult life. Maternal underweight before pregnancy (BMI <19.8 kg/m²) and low pregnancy weight gain increase risk of low infant birth weight.
Pre-pregnancy Preparation
  1. Achieve and maintain healthy body weight
  2. Choose an adequate and balanced diet
  3. Be physically active
  4. Receive regular medical care
  5. Manage chronic conditions
Energy & Nutrition Requirements
  • To meet basic metabolic needs
  • Blood production and cell growth
  • Fetal growth and development
  • Maternal red blood cell mass
  • DNA and new cell synthesis (Folate, Vitamin B12, Iron, Zinc)

Definition &amp; Objectives

DEFINITION
The postpartum period (puerperium) starts about two hours after the delivery of the placenta and continues for six weeks. It is the period when anatomic and physiologic changes of pregnancy are reversed, and the body returns to the normal non-pregnant state.
Objectives of Postnatal Care
👶 For the Newborn
  1. Early detection of any health problems
  2. Establishment and promotion of successful breast feeding
  3. Counseling and initiation of proper infant care by the mother and family
👩 For the Mother
  1. Reproductive health promotion (physical, psychological, social)
  2. Prevention of postpartum complications
  3. Early detection of complications and referral
  4. Psychological support by health care providers
  5. Counseling for support by husband and family
  6. Social adjustment and re-integration in family and society
  7. Counseling for contraception for spacing
  8. Providing appropriate health education messages

Postpartum Problems

Physical Problems

🩸 Postpartum Hemorrhage

Excessive bleeding after childbirth

🦠 Infections

Endometritis, UTI, wound infections (C-section or episiotomy sites)

🤱 Breastfeeding Issues

Mastitis, engorgement, cracked nipples, difficulty with latch or milk supply

🔴 Perineal Pain

Due to episiotomy or tearing during vaginal delivery

🤰 Pelvic Pain

Related to uterine involution or musculoskeletal changes

🚽 Urinary & Bowel Problems

Incontinence, constipation, hemorrhoids

😴 Fatigue & Anemia

Due to blood loss and demands of caring for a newborn

🦋 Thyroid Disorders

Postpartum thyroiditis → hyper- or hypothyroidism

💊 Contraception Needs

Counseling and initiation of appropriate birth control

Contraceptive Methods

1 Family Planning Overview

WHO Definition: Family planning is the ability of individuals and couples to anticipate and attain their desired number of children and the spacing and timing of their births.

Importance of Family Planning

High fertility can affect the health of mothers, present child, and coming child:

  • Mother: Malnutrition (IDA, osteomalacia), post-partum depression
  • Present child: Deprived of proper breastfeeding period and care — prone to malnutrition
  • Coming child: Mother lacks time to restore Iron and Calcium stores

The Four "TOO" — Target Groups for Contraception

Maternal and child mortality are increased by:

TOO Young< 20 years
TOO SoonInter-pregnancy spacing < 2 years
TOO Many5th pregnancy or more
TOO Old> 35 years
